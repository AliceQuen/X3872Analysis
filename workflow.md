0. Before you start the analysis, you need to do CMSSW to generate your data ntuple and MC ntuple.

    See [OniaMuMu](https://github.com/AliceQuen/Onia2MuMu.git) and
        [CRAB-Tool](https://cms-sw.github.io/CRAB/CRAB_3_1_0/CRAB_3_1_0.html) for more details.
1. Merge the ntuple.
2. Do Cut Optimization.
3. Find the best cut and see the performance, i.e. the merit, efficiency and signifcance.
4. Apply the cut to the data and mc ntuple.
5. Draw the plots.
6. Reweight MC ntuple.
7. Do TMVA and see performance.
